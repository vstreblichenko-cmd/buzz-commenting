Buzz Commenting

A global fan community, built one comment at a time.

Every day, inDrive’s brand accounts jump on the memes, trends and cultural moments people are talking about, while they are still hot. We leave witty, human comments under trending videos on TikTok, Instagram, Threads and Facebook. People like them, push them to the top and talk back.

But behind all this humor is a serious strategy.

Why we did it

Our own pages work as a support channel.

It comes with our category. When people are unhappy with service, they look for the brand’s social page to complain. When they are happy, they simply use the app every day.

We wanted to change that, and to build an internet-wide community of inDrive fans.

The idea

Go where people already are, find the ones who enjoy inDrive and give them a place to speak up.

So we show up in the comments under the videos they scroll for fun.

People share their good experiences on their own, and others join in. It lifts our NSS (Net Sentiment Score), grows the community and shows everyone how many happy users inDrive really has.

How we do it · Strategy

It looks like funny comments. Behind it is a system.

Organic likes per month, by publishing month2.4× January → September

This growth came without increasing investments.

October comments are still collecting likes: each comment is re-checked for three months.

Every comment has a type.

  • UnconnectedPure fun, no link to the brand
    → top of mind
  • ConnectedRides or delivery are the context of the joke
    → awareness
  • Key messageWhat the market needs now, e.g. offer your own fare, or support for our 360 campaigns
    → value for moneyand other metrics, targeted at the moment
Every market gets its own mix, depending on our market positioning and business goals.

Each type of comment boosts a different metric, and each works at a different level of efficiency. So for every country we find the mix that delivers the message and, at the same time, stays as efficient as possible. The same goes for platforms: we shift volume between TikTok, Instagram, Threads and Facebook depending on engagement results and NSS analytics in each market.
